Polynomial Formal Verification — Research Reimplementation

Research · In progress
BDDYOSYSAIGERPY-AIGERFORMAL

A DATE 2024 verification methodology rebuilt from the paper alone — the authors’ toolchain was never released. Published BDD node counts reproduced exactly on two instructions, and the paper’s complexity bound re-derived from my own measurements.

  • Per-instruction sub-circuit extraction by opcode pinning and constant propagation → AIG → ROBDD
  • AND = 97, XOR = 161 — exact matches against the published Table III
  • ADD per-bit growth measured as exactly linear, closed form 3k + 2, independently confirming the Θ(n²) bound
  • Found that BDD counts are unreproducible without a stated variable order — a bad order took the same circuit from 0.5 s to 7 GB and non-termination

Silicon · Complete
LIBRELANEOPENROADSKY130STADRC/LVS

The formally verified ALU hardened from RTL to routed silicon on the SkyWater open PDK — synthesized, placed, routed, and signed off across nine process-voltage-temperature corners. Not simulated. Not estimated. Laid out.

  • Full RTL-to-GDSII flow: synthesis → floorplan → placement → routing → RC extraction → nine-corner STA sign-off
  • Measured 31.5 MHz worst-case to 56.3 MHz best-case — a 1.8× PVT spread on identical silicon, characterized by linear constraint sweep
  • Zero DRC, LVS, and antenna violations; zero inferred latches confirmed by lint
  • Gate-level critical path traced with OpenSTA to a named next-iteration target: carry-lookahead adder

02

How every project is built

The same discipline runs through all five: a claim isn't a result until it's been checked by something that could have said no.

A

Self-checking first

Every testbench verifies its own results — golden-model comparison, scoreboards, assertion counts. No eyeballing waveforms and calling it done.

B

Formal where it matters

Safety properties proven with SymbiYosys, not just simulated. And proofs are audited for vacuousness — two vacuous proofs caught and fixed across this portfolio.

C

Real hardware, real numbers

FPGA validation on a Nexys A7 where possible; nine-corner STA on sky130 where it counts. Measured frequencies, not estimated ones.

D

Bugs are the story

The SRA false counterexample, the vacuous CDC proof, the fixed 31.7 ns critical path — each project documents what went wrong and how it was caught.
